# Dataset design and generating mechanisms

All records are fictional. Healthcare: one patient at one assessment, 1,000 independent identities. Latent inflammation, age, smoking, family history and exposure influence a probabilistic latent cancer-status outcome; diagnostic markers, symptoms and imaging are noisy descendants of status and shared latent factors. Biomarker measurements overlap across labels; nothing is a clinical measurement calibrated to real populations. Class prevalence is an emergent simulated value, not epidemiological prevalence. Symptoms and diagnostics are assumed measured at the assessment before the fictional reference label is revealed. Association is not causation.

Retail: 10 products × 100 consecutive days (2026-01-01 to 2026-04-10), one store, one product-store-day per row. Demand is Poisson with log-intensity depending on product category, price, discounts, advertising (log diminishing returns), weekend, promotion, customer rating, operational delay, common daily shocks and a stochastic temporal trend. Realized units = min(latent demand, inventory). Demand itself is not an observed target. Returns are drawn after sales. Gross revenue = units × unit_price; discount amount = gross × discount_pct; net revenue = gross − discount − returns × discounted unit price; gross margin = net revenue − (units − returns) × cost. Monetary units are fictional.

For one-day-ahead forecasting, the reference model uses only product identity, calendar and yesterday's sales plus a backward rolling seven-observation mean. It does not use future actual advertising, rating, delay, inventory or prices. Those are only valid future inputs if committed before forecast time or treated as hypothetical scenarios. Forecast horizon = one day; actual yesterday's sales must be observed before each next-day prediction. This is a rolling-origin evaluation, not a single fixed-origin multi-day forecast.

Healthcare partitions: stratified 700/150/150. Retail raw chronological partitions: 700/150/150 rows, 70/15/15 whole dates. Lag construction discards the initial 10 rows; resulting model partitions are 690/150/150. Temporal test remains untouched. Product-wise shifts prevent cross-product leakage.

Missingness: MCAR is randomized on BMI/advertising, MAR is conditioned on age/promotion, MNAR is conditioned on a hidden high biomarker or advertising value. The mechanism is known only because it was programmed. Do not assume MNAR is recoverable by standard imputation. Exact duplicate replacement occurs in the last two row positions; displaced identities are fully documented in the instructor manifest. Some raw defects are nonrecoverable without reference data. True target labels are never deliberately altered (retail target may have no MNAR corruption). Legitimate clean extremes must not be automatically discarded.

Statistical scales are different from pandas dtypes: Celsius is interval; age, BMI, price and sales are ratio; ordinal severity is not continuous; identifiers are not predictors. Review CSV dictionaries for each field. Synthetic correlations and model performance are not evidence of real-world validity.
